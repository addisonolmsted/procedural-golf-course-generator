# Landform dimensions: literature cross-check of the corpus zone statistics

**Why this exists** (user direction, 2026-08-22): the corpus is a biased
sampler — the clean-tile screen selected against farmed valley bottoms and
settled terraces, which is exactly where several landforms live. `zone_stats`
numbers are cross-checked here against published dimensions, and the records
note where literature overrides a thin corpus.

| landform | corpus (zone_stats) | literature | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fluvial terrace risers** (rv) | 5.2 / 7.2 / 7.9 m — tight cluster | typically **5–50 m**; treads tens of m to km; successive-terrace vertical spacing 10–100 m | corpus captures only the LOW young terraces (survivorship of the screen). Keep 7 m as the common case, allow an occasional tall riser. |
| **Terrace treads** (rv) | 32 / 128 / 576 m | hundreds of m to km in unconfined valleys; narrower + steeper risers in confined ones | corpus consistent with literature's lower-mid range ✓; widen the top of our tread range. |
| **Hill-country bluffs** (Edwards Plateau analog) | risers 5.6 / 19.6 / 85.8 m, 3.0/km | canyons incised 100–300 m into limestone; stepped valley walls; Balcones edge 200–300 m | corpus p90 (85.8) agrees with deep-canyon walls. Our 12–26 m bluffs sit in the real distribution ✓. |
| **Caprock / cuesta scarps** (plains) | 4.9 / 12.4 / 36.4 m | Flint Hills cuestas ~60 m; the Caprock Escarpment 60–300 m — but those are REGIONAL edges, ~10¹–10³ m over tens of km | at 3 km-tile scale the corpus (5–15 m local caprock steps) is the right number; the 60–300 m figures are a different (regional) scale. Our 5–15 m stands ✓. |
| **Sandhills dunes** | 7.4 m risers, 2.1/km — **way low** | barchans avg **41 m** high; barchanoid ridges to **90–150 m**, 3–8 km long | the corpus's clean-tile screen kept the gentle tiles, and trunk-transects are the wrong instrument for dunes. **Literature governs T4's aeolian generator**: dune relief tens of metres, ridge spacing km-scale — bounded by golf (10–35 m playable dune relief). |
| **Kettle holes** (heathland, T4) | not yet measured | 5 m–13 km diameter (typically tens–hundreds of m); depths to 45 m, commonly a few–15 m | T4 dial: diameter 40–400 m, depth 2–12 m, golf-bounded. |

**Method note:** corpus stays authoritative where its sampling is sound
(piedmont, hc riser distribution, plains at tile scale); literature overrides
where the screen demonstrably censored the landform (rv tall risers,
sandhills dunes); golf bounds both (03-macro-is-designed).

Sources: Limaye & Lamb 2016 (JGR-ES, autogenic terraces); Springer *Encyclopedia
of Planetary Landforms* (valley terrace); Wikipedia "Fluvial terrace";
Britannica "Caprock Escarpment"; Wikipedia "Caprock Escarpment", "Cuesta",
"Texas Hill Country"; American Southwest Virtual Museum "Edwards Plateau";
